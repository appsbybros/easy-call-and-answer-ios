A familiar face. A little closer.

Easy Call gives your favorite people a calm, easy-to-read home on your iPhone. Find a name or photo, tap Call, and continue in Apple's familiar Phone app.

MADE FOR COMFORT
• Large contact cards and clear, generously sized controls.
• Choose a person from Contacts or add a name and number yourself.
• Add a useful note: an extension, a reference number, or the best time to call.
• A spacious keypad, larger text support and VoiceOver labels.
• Optional stronger contrast and a simpler home screen.

A LITTLE HELP, WHEN YOU NEED IT
Find straightforward guidance for full-screen incoming calls, Apple's Assistive Access, speaker routing and Bluetooth. Prepare your car connection while parked, then use Siri or your vehicle's controls.

FREE CALLING. OPTIONAL EXTRAS.
Calling, the keypad and four favorite people are free. Start a free 14-day Trial through Apple to try extra favorites and call reminders. It starts only after your confirmation, never renews, and never turns into an automatic charge. After 14 days, adding extra favorites and new reminders requires the lifetime upgrade. One optional lifetime purchase unlocks up to 100 favorites and local reminders. Contacts already saved stay callable after the preview ends.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, ads or tracking SDKs. Favorite contacts and notes stay on your device; your Apple backup settings may include them in a device backup. Apple processes purchases. Optional reminder notifications may display a contact's name according to your notification preview settings.

HOW CALLS WORK
Easy Call opens Apple's Phone app for ordinary calls. Answering and ending happen on Apple's call screen. Easy Call does not replace incoming cellular controls, record calls, or automatically dial. FaceTime Audio is also available when supported. Carrier charges and device availability apply. iPad can use supported FaceTime calling; it is not a standalone cellular telephone.
