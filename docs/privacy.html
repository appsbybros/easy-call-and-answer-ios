Privacy policy · effective 8 October 2026

What stays on your device

Easy Call saves the contact names, telephone numbers, optional photos, notes and reminders you choose, plus your display preferences and local preview dates. This information stays in the app's local storage. We operate no contact-upload server, advertising SDK or analytics SDK in this iOS app. We do not read call history, record calls or use your microphone.

The system Contacts picker shares only the contact information you select. The app does not request full address-book access. Optional reminder notifications may display a person's name on your lock screen; use the iPhone notification preview settings to control that.

Apple services

Apple handles purchases and the app reads verified purchase entitlements to unlock features. Phone and FaceTime calls use Apple's apps and your carrier where applicable. Device backups may include local app data, depending on your Apple backup settings. These services have their own privacy policies.

Your choices and deletion

Remove a person from their detail screen to delete their saved information and associated reminders in Easy Call. This does not delete their entry in Apple Contacts. You can cancel reminders individually. Deleting Easy Call removes its local storage. Manage device backups separately through Apple. No Easy Call account exists to close.

Support

If you choose to email us, we receive the email address and information you send to respond to your request. Please do not send contact lists, call recordings, or unnecessary sensitive details. You may request deletion of support correspondence; we retain only correspondence needed to resolve support, comply with applicable obligations, or establish legitimate records.

Contact and changes

Apps by Bros · support@easycallandanswer.com. We will update the effective date if this policy changes. This policy covers Easy Call for iOS, not the separate Android application.
